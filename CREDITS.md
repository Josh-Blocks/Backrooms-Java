# Credits & attributions

Josh's Blocks: Backrooms is an original work. The *concept* of a "backrooms"
liminal space is a widely-shared internet genre; this mod is our own clean-room
interpretation of it — our own code, our own art, our own name. No code, assets
or designs are taken from any other Minecraft mod, paid or free.

## Third-party assets

None yet. Every texture, model and sound shipped in this mod is original to
Josh's Blocks.

If that ever changes, each third-party asset must be listed here with its source
and licence before it ships — MIT/Apache-2.0 (with attribution) only. Anything
"All Rights Reserved" is never used.
